# SEO Audit — places-egy.com — 2026-09-04

## Executive summary

The site's SEO foundation is 70% solid: HTTPS/HSTS/CSP are clean, robots.txt intelligently welcomes AI crawlers, the sitemap is served, per-listing detail pages and per-developer pages carry unique titles + canonicals, and a well-formed `RealEstateAgent` + `WebSite` schema pair ships on every prerendered page. That's a healthier baseline than most Cairo real-estate competitors.

However, three high-value page families are silently broken in Google's eyes. **Location pages (`/locations/{slug}`), the FAQ hub (`/faqs/`), and the Guides hub (`/guides/`) are not prerendered** — Googlebot receives the homepage `<title>`, meta description, and canonical (pointing to `/`), which effectively deindexes them and creates a duplicate-content signal against the homepage. The sheraton location page is entirely empty. Plus, guide pages ship no `Article` or `BreadcrumbList` schema, and FAQ pages ship no `FAQPage` schema in the pre-JS HTML (they only mount after React hydrates, which Google may or may not execute). Top-3 fix priorities: (1) prerender the 6 location pages + `/faqs/` + `/guides/` hubs; (2) add `Article` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD to guides and `FAQPage` JSON-LD to prerendered FAQ HTML; (3) purge retired `/admin/*` URLs and refresh `<lastmod>` in `sitemap.xml`.

## What's healthy ✅

- **Unique titles + canonicals + OG tags** on Home, /listings hub, /developers hub, per-developer pages, per-guide pages, per-listing detail pages, and per-FAQ-section pages.
- **Security + HTTPS + HSTS** fully configured (`strict-transport-security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`).
- **robots.txt is smart** — explicitly welcomes GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, etc., and blocks the `/listings?…` filter noise that was causing "Redirect error" reports in GSC.
- **Site-wide JSON-LD**: valid `RealEstateAgent` (NAP: Cairo, `+201063618217`, `admin@places-egy.com`, `areaServed` covers all 6 districts, `sameAs` links IG/FB/LI/TT) + `WebSite` with `SearchAction`.
- **Sitemap.xml** exists with 165 URLs, referenced from robots.txt.
- **WordPress blog subdomain properly deindexed** (per prior work).
- **www → non-www 301** live at Cloudflare.
- **Per-listing detail pages** carry rich titles like "Premium Office 1,570 SQM Fully Finished Office Space for Lease at Mivida Business Park, New Cairo | PLACES" with correct canonicals — this is the money page for long-tail rankings and it's set up right.

## Critical issues 🚨

### 1. Location pages are NOT prerendered — Google sees the homepage
**What.** `/locations/new-cairo`, `/sheikh-zayed`, `/6th-october`, `/downtown-cairo`, `/sheraton`, `/maadi` all return the homepage `<title>` ("Buy & Rent Admin Offices Across Egypt | PLACES"), the homepage meta description, and `<link rel="canonical" href="https://places-egy.com/" />`. Verified by curl on all 6 slugs.
**Why.** These are the single highest-intent commercial pages on the site — "office space new cairo" is the exact query a buyer types. Right now Google is told they're duplicates of the homepage and won't rank them. The self-referencing canonical to `/` actively tells Google to consolidate their signal into the homepage.
**Fix.** Extend `script/prerender.ts` to loop over the 6 location slugs (same pattern as the developer-page loop already there) and emit `dist/public/locations/<slug>.html` with per-location title, description, canonical (`https://places-egy.com/locations/<slug>`), and OG tags. The developer-page block in `script/prerender.ts` is the template to copy.

### 2. `/locations/sheraton` returns a blank shell with no title at all
**What.** curl returns HTTP 200 but `<title>` is empty — the head template has no fallback title for this route.
**Why.** Users can arrive from the sitemap, from social shares, from GSC — and every one of them sees a nameless tab.
**Fix.** Same prerender fix as #1 covers it. Verify by re-curling after deploy.

### 3. `/guides/` and `/faqs/` hub pages are unindexable
**What.** Both hubs return `<title>Buy & Rent Admin Offices Across Egypt | PLACES</title>` and canonical `/`. Also `/guides` and `/faqs` (no trailing slash) 301 to the trailing-slash versions, which is fine but wasteful — link builders may hit the non-slash form.
**Why.** These are the two content pillars that should rank for "Cairo office guide", "office rental FAQ Egypt", etc. Right now they're invisible to search.
**Fix.** In `script/prerender.ts`, add `/guides/` and `/faqs/` to the "index shells" list alongside `/listings` and `/developers` (that block already exists).

### 4. No `Article` or `BreadcrumbList` schema on guides
**What.** `/guides/office-rental-prices-cairo-2026` ships only the site-wide `RealEstateAgent` + `WebSite` schema. No `Article`, no `author`, no `datePublished`, no breadcrumbs.
**Why.** Guides are 1,780+ word data-backed pieces (verified in `client/src/pages/guides/office-rental-prices-cairo-2026.tsx`) — exactly the content Google's Helpful Content system wants to reward with rich results. Without `Article` schema they can't earn article-carousel or top-stories placement, and without `BreadcrumbList` the SERP breadcrumb line doesn't render.
**Fix.** In the guide loop inside `script/prerender.ts`, inject two JSON-LD blocks per guide: `Article` (with `headline`, `description`, `author: {"@type":"Organization","name":"PLACES"}`, `datePublished`, `image`) and `BreadcrumbList` (Home → Guides → Guide title). Data source: `client/src/lib/guides.ts` already has slug/title/description/imageUrl.

### 5. No `FAQPage` schema in pre-JS HTML on FAQ pages
**What.** `client/src/pages/resources/faqs-section.tsx` renders `FAQPage` JSON-LD, but that only appears in the DOM after React hydrates. curl of `/faqs/office-rental-basics` shows only the site-wide schema.
**Why.** FAQ rich results (the accordion snippet in Google) require the schema in the initial HTML. Google's rendering is inconsistent for JS-injected structured data.
**Fix.** In the FAQ loop inside `script/prerender.ts`, read the section's Q/A array from the same source file the React component reads (`client/src/lib/faqs.ts` or wherever `sections` is defined) and inject `FAQPage` JSON-LD into the prerendered `<head>`.

## Medium issues ⚠️

### 6. Sitemap still lists retired `/admin/*` category URLs
**What.** `sitemap.xml` includes `/admin`, `/admin/business-park`, `/admin/business-building`, `/admin/mixed-use`. These paths are the admin dashboard route family (per prior redirect work these should now 301 elsewhere or be private).
**Why.** Sitemap URLs that 301 or 404 waste crawl budget and drop overall sitemap trust score.
**Fix.** Edit `script/build-sitemap.ts` (or the static block, depending on which is source of truth) — remove the four `/admin*` entries. Re-run the sitemap build.

### 7. `<lastmod>` in sitemap is `2026-07-10` for every URL
**What.** Every URL carries the same 2-month-old `lastmod`. Listings that changed yesterday still say July 10.
**Why.** Google uses `lastmod` to prioritize recrawls. A frozen date means Google won't refresh fast-moving listing pages promptly.
**Fix.** Have `script/build-sitemap.ts` set `lastmod` from the actual file mtime for static routes and from the listing's `updated_at` column for `/listings/<id>-<slug>` URLs.

### 8. Home HTML body is an empty `<div id="root">` — zero internal links crawlable pre-JS
**What.** Stripping scripts from the homepage HTML leaves ~45 bytes: just the root div. Googlebot can render JS, but its crawl is delayed and imperfect. Bing, Perplexity, and older AI crawlers often skip JS entirely.
**Why.** With no anchor tags in raw HTML, non-JS crawlers can't discover `/listings`, `/guides`, `/developers`, `/locations/*`, `/faqs/`. Sitemap coverage partially compensates but internal linking anchor text is a major ranking signal that's just missing.
**Fix.** Cheap version: append a hidden `<nav>` in `client/index.html` (or in the prerender template) with anchor links to the top ~15 pages (6 locations + guides hub + FAQ hub + developers hub + listings + top 3 guides + top 3 developers). Not `display:none` (Google may ignore) — use a visible-but-minimal footer already in the shell, or an `aria-hidden` skip-nav in the header. Better version: SSR the actual header/footer at prerender time.

### 9. `og:image` is the same generic `/opengraph.jpg` on every page
**What.** Home, listings, guides, developers, all share `opengraph.jpg`. Per-developer and per-guide pages have unique titles but no unique OG image.
**Why.** Social shares (LinkedIn especially, where a Cairo B2B office broker lives) look thin without page-specific imagery, and CTR from Slack/WhatsApp previews suffers.
**Fix.** In prerender, override `og:image` for per-guide pages with `guide.imageUrl` (already in `client/src/lib/guides.ts`) and for per-developer pages with the developer logo. Same for listing detail pages (use first listing photo).

### 10. Locations content is duplicated between `/locations/{slug}` and homepage sections
**What.** The homepage has location cards, and `/locations/{slug}` renders a location detail page. Since locations aren't prerendered (#1) they currently share the homepage's meta — actual duplication. Once fixed by #1, verify the H1 + first-paragraph copy on each `/locations/<slug>` is unique to that district (not templated word-for-word).
**Fix.** After #1 is deployed, spot-check `/locations/new-cairo` vs `/locations/sheikh-zayed` — first 300 rendered words should differ substantively.

## Low priority / polish

### 11. No `hreflang` tags
`og:locale` says `en_EG` with `og:locale:alternate ar_EG`, but no Arabic version of pages exists yet. Add `hreflang` only if/when Arabic pages ship. Skip for now.

### 12. First-visit TTFB was 3.5s (Cloudflare cold cache)
Subsequent hits were 500–800ms. Likely a cold-origin bundle. Watch Cloudflare "cache hit ratio" for `/` — if <50%, consider a longer edge-cache TTL on the prerendered HTML (currently `cache-control: no-cache`).

### 13. Add `Person` or `Organization` `author` to guides once #4 ships
Small trust signal for E-E-A-T. `"author": {"@type": "Organization", "name": "PLACES", "url": "https://places-egy.com"}` suffices.

### 14. No BreadcrumbList on developer or listing detail pages
Same reasoning as #4. Low priority; ship after #4.

### 15. No GSC-visible `google-site-verification` meta in HTML head
Verify GSC is set up (likely done via DNS TXT). If DNS-based, ignore. If not, add the meta tag.
